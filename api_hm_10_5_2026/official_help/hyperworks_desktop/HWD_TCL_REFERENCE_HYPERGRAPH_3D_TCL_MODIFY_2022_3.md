| [hwI3DViewCtrl Zoom](https://2022.help.altair.com/2022.3/hwdesktop/hwd/topics/reference/tcl/hwi3dviewctrl_zoom.htm) | HyperGraph 3D Tcl Modify | `hwI3DViewControl_handle Zoom factor` | This command zooms in or out of the graph based on the factor that you input. | **Inputs:** factor A user-input number that results in a zoom in or out of the plot. If the factor is a positive number greater than 1, the plot zooms in the amount of times indicated by the factor. If the factor is a positive number less than 1, the plot zooms out to the factor times the original view. A factor of a negative number or a factor of 1 does not result in a zoom change. |
