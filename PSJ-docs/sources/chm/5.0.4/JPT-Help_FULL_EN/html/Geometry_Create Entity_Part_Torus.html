    • - Operating Procedure -

    1.  Enter the part name in the Parts Name.
    2.  Specify the origin coordinates in Origin-X, Y, Z.
    3.  Enter the radius of the inner circle in Inner Radius.
    4.  Enter the radius of the ring in Ring radius.
    5.  Enter the number of divisions to be generated on the circumference of the ring in Number of Circum Nodes.
    6.  Enter the number of divisions to be generated on the cross-section of the ring in Number of Ring Nodes.
    7.  Click the Apply / OK button.
