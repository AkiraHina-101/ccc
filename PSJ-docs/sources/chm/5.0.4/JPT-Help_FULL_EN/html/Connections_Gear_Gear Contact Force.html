Gear | Gear Contact Force

Set the gear transferred conditions made by Connections and Local Coordinate.
Command will create a group after launch.
Limitation : Gear Parts should be Sweep Hexa Mesh.

Gear Contact Force

  • Name

    Enter the name of the item to be created.

    Rotation Axis

  • Direction of Rotation Axis

    Specify the axis rotation of the gear.

  • Coordinate

    Specify the reference coordinate for Direction of Rotation Axis.

    Drive

  • Direction of Rotation

    Specify the direction of rotation of the drive gear. (Base on Direction of Rotation Axis)

  • Num. Teeth

    Enter the number of teeths of drive gear.

  • Direction of Torsion

    Specify the direction of torsion of the helical gear. (Base on Direction of Rotation Axis)

  • Torsion Angle(deg)

    Enter the torsion angle of the helical gear.

  • Pressure Angle(deg)

    Enter the pressure angle of the gear.

    • Driven

  • Num. Teeth

    Enter the number of teeths of the driven gear.

    • - Operating Procedure -

    1.  Select two edges of the cylinder face of the gear in the order of drive gear - driven gear.
    2.  Specify the Direction of Rotaion Axis.
    3.  Change options in Drive or Driven tap as needed.
    4.  Click Apply or OK button.
