Scaling

Enlarge / reduce the selected Part.

Operation

  • Create New Part

    •    Copy the Part after Scaling, as a new Part.

  • LBC Copy

    •    Also copy load constraints that were added to the existing part to a new part.

    Scaling

  • Coordinate System

    •    Select the coordinate system to reference.

  • X

    •    Enter the enlargement / reduction ratio in the X-axis direction of the selected coordinate system.

  • Y

    •    Enter the enlargement / reduction ratio in the Y-axis direction of the selected coordinate system.

  • Z

    •    Enter the enlargement / reduction ratio in the Z-axis direction of the selected coordinate system.

  • Use Part Center

    •    If you check the box, center position of the Part will be at the center for Scaling.

  • Center-X

    •    Enter the X coordinate of the center position for Scaling.

  • Center-Y

    •    Enter the Y coordinate of the center position for Scaling.

  • Center-Z

    •    Enter the Z coordinate of the center position for Scaling.


    - Operating Procedure -

    1.  Select the Create New Part if you want to create a new part after Scaling.
    2.  Turn on the LBC Copy, to copy a load restraint conditions from the existing Part to the new part.
    3.  Press the Part button and select the Part for Scaling from the screen.
    4.  Select the coordinate system from Coordinate System, to refer to Scaling.
    5.  Enter the coefficient for Scaling X, Y, to Z.
    6.  Select check box for Part center, if you want to adjust the position of the center of Scaling to the center location for Part.
    7.  If you do not want alignment with the center location for Part, turn off the Use Part Center to enter the coordinate value of the center location in the center -X, center -Y, center -Z.
    8.  Press the Apply or OK button.
