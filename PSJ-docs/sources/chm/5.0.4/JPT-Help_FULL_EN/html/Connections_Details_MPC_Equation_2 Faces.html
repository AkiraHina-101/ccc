MPC Equation | 2 Faces

It is the ability to create MPC between multiple points of two faces.

Faces-Faces

  • Name

    Enter the name to be registered.

    • Parameters

  • Coordinate

    Select the reference coordinate system.

    • MPC Term

  • Coef1(Slave)

    Enter the coefficients of the slave.

  • DOF1(Slave)

    Choose the degree of freedom of the slave.

  • Coef2(Master)

    Enter the coefficients of the master.

  • DOF2(Master)

    Choose the degree of freedom of the master.

    • - Operating Procedure -

    1.  Select the master face.
    2.  Select the slave face.
    3.  Set the parameters.
    4.  Click Apply / OK button.
