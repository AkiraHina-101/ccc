View Control

Control the current view by numerical input.

Center

The input coordinates are taken as the rotation center of the model.

  • Reset

    • The rotation center is reset. ※The numerical value being input is not reset.

    Rotate

    Rotate by the input angle around each axis.

    Pan

    Translate (pan) the view.

  • Pan in

    Move to the right or up by the entered number.

  • Pan out

    Move left or down by the entered number.

    • Zoom

    The view is zoomed in / out at the input magnification rate.

  • Fit

    Click the button to fit the entire model to the size of the main window.

    • - Operation Procedure (Center) -

    1.  Enter coordinate values in x / y / z.
    2.  Click the "Set" button.

    - Operation Procedure (Rotate) -

    1.  Enter the rotation angle in x / y / z.
    2.  Click the "Rotate" button.

    - Operation Procedure (Pan) -

    1.  Enter the movement amount to the right / up.
    2.  Click the "Pan In" or "Pan Out" button.

    - Operation Procedure (Zoom) -

    1.  Enter the magnification to zoom in or out.
    2.  Click the "Zoom In" or "Zoom Out" button.
    3.  Click the "Fit" button to fit the entire model to the size of the main window.
