IN Boundary | Limited Boundary | Plane

Create a limited boundary plane.

Plane

  • Plane On

    •    Specify the coordinate plane of the global coordinate system.

  • Center Point

    •    Specify the center point of the plane.

  • Length1

    •    The length of the edge 1 of the plane.

  • Length2

    •    The length of the edge 2 of the plane.

    - Operating Procedure -

    1.  Select the infinite boundary surface part.
    2.  Enter the center point, length 1, and length 2.
    3.  Click the Apply/OK button.
