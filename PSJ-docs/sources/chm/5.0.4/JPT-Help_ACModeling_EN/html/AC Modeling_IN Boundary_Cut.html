In Boundary｜Cut

Cut at the restricted plane of the infinite boundary plane.

Boundary cut

- Operating procedure -

  1.  Select two parts, the infinite boundary and the restricted surface.
  2.  Execute with the Apply/OK button.
  3.  It is joined into one part and edge lines are created at the intersections of the faces.
  4.  Select the face you don't need and remove it from the Delete menu.
